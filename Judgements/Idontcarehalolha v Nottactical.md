**INTRODUCTION**
[1] This matter concerns a civil action brought by the Plaintiff, Idontcarehalolha, against the Defendant, Nottactical, for Improper Agency Action and Procedural Misconduct.

[2] The Plaintiff alleges that on January 18, 2026, the Defendant engaged in improper custodial conduct and irregular administrative actions during arrest, detention, and transport procedures, resulting in harm and administrative penalties to the Plaintiff.

[3] A Notice of Civil Claim was filed with the Court and, pursuant to Rule 201(B) of the GRP, the Plaintiff was responsible for serving the Notice of Claim upon the Defendant.

[4] The evidence before the Court confirms that the Notice of Claim was served upon the Defendant via direct message on Discord. Rule 201(B) requires that the Plaintiff serve the Notice of Claim; it does not require acknowledgment, confirmation of opening, or acceptance by the Defendant.

[5] The Defendant was actively using the Discord platform at the time service was effected. The Court is satisfied that the direct message was successfully delivered to the Defendant’s account.

[6] The Defendant was temporarily banned from the server; however, records confirm that the Defendant was unbanned on January 22, 2026, and rejoined the server. The Defendant remained in the server thereafter.

[7] Default was entered on January 29, 2026, seven (7) days after the Defendant regained access to the server.

[8] The Notice of Claim clearly stated that the Defendant was required to file a Reply within forty-eight (48) hours when prompted by the Court.

[9] The Defendant did not file a Reply within the required timeframe, did not seek an extension, and did not bring a timely motion to set aside service.

**FINDINGS**
[10] The Court finds that service under Rule 201(B) was properly effected via direct message. The rule requires service; it does not require acknowledgment or confirmation of reading.

[11] The Defendant’s temporary inability to access the server does not invalidate service by direct message.

[12] The Defendant regained full access to the server on January 22, 2026, and had sufficient opportunity to respond prior to default being entered on January 29, 2026.

[13] The Court is satisfied that the Defendant had notice of the proceedings and failed to respond within the prescribed timeline.

[14] The Defendant’s failure to file a Reply constitutes non-compliance with procedural requirements and places the Defendant in default.

[15] Based on the uncontested evidence submitted by the Plaintiff, and in the absence of a filed Reply, the Court accepts the Plaintiff’s evidence as unchallenged.

**JUDGEMENTS AND ORDERS**

[16] The default judgment entered on January 29, 2026, remains in full force and effect.

[17] The Plaintiff is awarded 125,000 CAD in compensatory damages.

[18] The Defendant is granted an additional seven (7) days from the date of this Order to satisfy the monetary award previously ordered.

[19] The Defendant must provide proof of full payment to the Court within seven (7) days of this order.

[20] Failure to comply with this order within seven (7) days shall result in the issuance of a warrant without further notice.
